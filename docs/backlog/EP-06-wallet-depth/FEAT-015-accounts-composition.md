# FEAT-015 — Accounts: composition breakdown and sparklines

**What.** A composition breakdown of where net worth sits, plus a 30-day sparkline on each account card.

**Why now.** Account cards show a balance and nothing about its direction. A sparkline answers "is this going up or down" without opening the account.

**Spec.** [wallet/03-feature-waves.md](../../roadmap/design-adoption/wallet/03-feature-waves.md) — the design work is already done; this item tracks *whether* to build it, not how.
